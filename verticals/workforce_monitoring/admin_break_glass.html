🚨 Emergency Access

Break-glass grants for Insight Health Solutions technical staff. Under document access rules, IHS technical staff cannot open document content through any screen or interface — a support incident that genuinely requires it needs a grant recorded here, by the program, in advance.
How a grant works. It names one IHS technical person and the specific documents they may open — view and download only, never edit. Only the Medical Director can record a grant; either the Medical Director or the Program Administrator can revoke one, effective immediately. It expires by itself in 24 hours, every document opened under it is written to the audit trail as its own emergency-access event, and both officers are notified the moment it is recorded. There is no deputy and no out-of-band path — if the Medical Director is unavailable, no grant can be recorded and support proceeds without content access. The honest boundary: these rules lock every screen and interface the platform has; the hosting database credentials themselves are governed by IHS's operating commitment and the audit trail, not by code.
Recording a grant belongs to the Medical Director alone. Your role can review the record below and revoke an active grant, but not record a new one — that is deliberate: the break-glass gate is the control standing behind every other control.
