Subject: Insight platform update, August 2026

Hello all,

This is the first of the monthly progress updates we committed to, and since our kickoff meeting is coming up, it doubles as a pre-kickoff picture of where things stand.

The exploration environment is ready for the August 13 orientation. It is a complete working copy of the platform, set to Pacific time, loaded with fictional participants whose stories are built to show real workflows: intake and review, the weekly check-in instruments, risk scoring, and the alerting and registry safety net behind them. Chris, Kellie, and Samantha each have their own login waiting. Nothing in it is real data, so your team can explore freely, click anything, and break nothing.

Washington's own production environment also exists on the platform. It is configured with the five Washington licensing boards and deliberately holds no placeholder data. The real configuration, your health systems and clinic list, gets entered at kickoff so that everything in it is true from day one.

Progress on the platform since the agreement was signed, in plain terms. Document access control is built: access to participant documents is now governed by role and confidentiality tier, deny by default, with a complete audit trail written before any document is viewed, downloaded, or exported, and an emergency-access procedure that locks vendor technical staff out of clinical content unless the program itself grants time-limited, named, fully audited access. The outbound messaging foundation and the engagement engine are in place. And the safety-detector layer, the part of the system that watches survey patterns and files alerts, has been audited end to end and proven to work identically on a second program's configuration, which is the heart of what makes the platform portable between states.

The road ahead is unchanged: kickoff by mid-August, a functional pilot targeted for mid-October with technical validation, workflow testing, and validation of the data migration from RecoveryTrek, and full production by June 30, 2027.

What we will ask of your team at kickoff: confirmation of the licensing board names, your list of health systems and clinics, and a first conversation about the RecoveryTrek data migration and lab integration so both can be scoped properly.

We are looking forward to the orientation on the 13th.

Bill
